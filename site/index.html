Voice Chat for Beat Saber 1.40.8

Talk with other players in multiplayer lobbies. Players talk directly through the game's own multiplayer connection, so there is no server to run or sign up for.

Download for Quest (.qmod) Download for PC (.zip) How to install

Features

  • Voice in multiplayer lobbies, with anyone else who has the mod.
  • Quest and PC cross-play. Quest and PC players in the same lobby can hear each other.
  • Mic button off to your left in the lobby, showing MIC ON or MIC OFF. Press it to toggle.
  • Settings: turn voice chat off, start muted, or use push-to-talk on a controller button.
  • No voice server needed. Voice is sent as packets over the game's multiplayer connection (needs a MultiplayerCore server such as BeatTogether).

Install

Quest

  1. Mod Beat Saber 1.40.8 with MBF.
  2. Upload the .qmod above in MBF. It installs MultiplayerCore, BSML and the other dependencies automatically.
  3. Join a multiplayer lobby and look to your left for the mic button. Settings are in the main menu's Voice Chat button.

PC

  1. Mod Beat Saber 1.40.8 and install BSML, SiraUtil and MultiplayerCore with your mod manager.
  2. Extract the .zip above into your Beat Saber folder so VoiceChat.dll ends up in Plugins.
  3. Join a multiplayer lobby and look to your left for the mic button. Settings are under Mod Settings > Voice Chat.

Privacy

Voice goes directly between players in the lobby and is not encrypted end to end. Only talk with people you trust. Don't rely on this for secure communication.
